What it does

01

The day, on one screen

Every appointment, who is waiting, who is in consultation, and which visits ended without payment. A receptionist reads it standing up.

02

Records that survive the owner

Visits, weights, vaccinations and files under one patient, not one phone number. Certificates print in black on white and photocopy cleanly.

03

Recalls that a person sends

Qvety works out who is due and writes the message. Your staff open WhatsApp on their own phone and press send. Nothing goes out automatically.

04

Money you can close out

Invoices in EGP with Western digits so they match the receipt printer, the bank, and the client's phone. Unpaid balances stay visible until they are not.

Arabic first, not Arabic later

The interface was laid out right-to-left and then adapted to English, not the other way round. Every table, form and invoice mirrors; the logo does not. Each user picks their own language — the receptionist can work in Arabic while the owner reads reports in English, on the same clinic, on the same data.

Numbers stay 0–9 in both languages, because that is what the printer, the bank and the client's phone show.
