Qvety

Brand and interface guidelines

Practice management software for Egyptian veterinary clinics. Arabic default, English per user, Angular and NG-ZORRO. Three words, in order: calm, clear, trustworthy.

01  /  Mark

Cut Q

A solid disc with the counter and the tail both cut out of it. The tail is a void leaving the bowl at 45°, terminal rounded, so it reads as a pad or a drop without drawing either. Nothing inside the bowl. One colour, always.

Real sizes

48 / 32 / 24 / 16 px. 16 px on screen and 6 mm in print are the floor. Below the floor there is no smaller variant — the mark simply is not used.

Clear space

Clear space on all sides equals the height of the bowl. Nothing — rule, edge, caption, another logo — enters it.

Never
  • Mirror the mark. A flipped tail is a different letter.
  • Add a cross, stethoscope, heart or heartbeat line.
  • Gradient, two-tone, outline or drop shadow.
  • Put anything inside the bowl.
  • Rebuild it at a different stroke weight.

The mark is never mirrored. Only the order of mark and wordmark changes between LTR and RTL. Minimum wordmark height is 12 px on screen, 4 mm in print; below that, mark only.

Open item. The wordmark here is stock IBM Plex Sans Medium. Its Q has a short straight tail and does not match the mark's 45° void. The Q needs redrawing by hand, and all wordmark text converting to outlines, before the lockup files ship.

Disabled is grey, never pale teal

On a cheap front-desk monitor a lighter teal is indistinguishable from the live teal. Disabled buttons and inputs take a line grey fill with ink-muted text.

Clinical states vs. money states
Vaccinated In consultation Unpaid EGP 450 Recall due Archived

Teal and green carry clinical state. Amber carries money and reminders. A receptionist should be able to tell which kind of problem a row has without reading it.

05  /  Icons

Everything generic — search, edit, print, calendar, user — comes from the Ant Design outlined set and is not redrawn. Custom icons exist only where Ant has nothing: 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, rounded caps and joins, outlined, single colour.

Directional icons (back, next, send) are mirrored by the app in RTL. Object icons — syringe, scale, animal heads — are not. Draw animal heads facing left; a cat facing right in Arabic is fine.
