16 px in a real tab

1b and 1c hold. 1a's counter starts to fill in below 20 px. 1d's toes merge into the pad and it becomes a notched disc.

Photocopy test

Solid-mass marks copy better than strokes. 1c is safest; 1a is the one I'd want to see on a real clinic copier before committing.

LTR vs RTL weight

The mark is never mirrored, so its tail always points down-right. In RTL the tail points away from the wordmark, which reads slightly lighter. Fix is clear space, not a flip.

Wordmark Q

These use stock IBM Plex Sans. Its Q tail is short and straight — close to 1a, wrong for 1c. Whichever mark wins, the wordmark's Q needs redrawing by hand to match.
